Gathered here are documents from MAT2717 - Stochastic processes, a class I taught during the 2022 fall and 2023 summer semesters.

the syllabus

T.A. budget

 Every semester, before the start of term, I made a budget for the TAs' contracted hours, including fixed commitments like tutorials, office hours and preparation, as well as grading. I would share this with TAs to make sure they understood what was expected of them, and that they were not being overworked.

selected homework assignments

For both the fall 2022 and summer 2023 semesters there were four homework assignments. Each one related to topics seen in class, but some also explored related mathematical methods not covered in class. In these instances, guidance was provided in the homework assignment. All of them also aimed at making connections with applied problems in other fields such as biology, ecology or finance. Here are four selected examples.

summer 2023, hw1 : monopoly

This homework assignment explores the board game Monopoly as a discrete-time, discrete-space Markov chain on 42 states roughly corresponding with the squares on the board. With a one-step transition probability matrix calculated by accounting for the distribution of dice rolls as well as the "Chance" and "Community chest" cards, and which was provided in the form of a spreadsheet, students were tasked to deduce elementary properties for this stochastic process, including its stationary distribution, and to make deductions applicable to strategy in the game.

winter 2022, hw3 : coalescent processes in phylogenetics

 This homework assignment on the topic of continuous-time, discrete space Markov chains explores coalescence models in the context of the study of the evolution of genes and species.

summer 2023, hw3 : forest fires

This assignment served as an introduction to renewal theory, in which students were guided through the modeling of a sample of trees to understand how fire (the main driver for the renewal of the Canadian boreal forest) affects the distribution of tree ages over time.

summer 2023, hw4 : the Black-Scholes equation

In this homework assignment covering martingales, students were taken through a simple derivation of one of mathematical finance's foundational results: the Black-Scholes formula for the pricing of options in a fair market.
