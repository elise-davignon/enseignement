Gathered here are some documents from the MAT2450 - Mathematics and technology class I taught in the winter semester of 2024. This class was aimed primarily at mathematics education students, but ended up being attended by a variety of students. It covered various applications of linear and elementary algebra, as well as some analysis.

This course comprised a final project that included a term paper and an oral presentation, to be done in teams.

During this course, I presented course notes in the form of interactive Mathematica notebooks that students could play around with and edit to explore the notions by themselves.

geometry and positioning

The first unit of the course was concerned with the geometry used in location technologies like GPS. The first notebook shows how elementary triangulation works; the second one shows how similar principles can be applied to the slightly harder problem of locating a signal like a lightning strike using antennae.
