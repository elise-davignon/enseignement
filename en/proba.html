the syllabus

selected homework assignments

In this class I gave out six homework assignments. Every one aimed at practicing notions seen in class while exploring interesting connections, either to more advanced mathematics, or to other topics like physics or computer science. Here are three examples.

hw1: Dyck's paths

 This was the first homework assignment, using notions of combinatorics, and it led students to derive a formula for Catalan numbers by counting Dyck's paths

hw3: Shannon entropy

This homework assignment focused on discrete random variables, and guided students through a small conceptual proof related to information theory.

hw4: The pendulum

 In this homework assignment, students derived the distribution for the position of a pendulum at a random moment in time uniformly distributed throughout the pendulum's period. An epilog to the assignment makes a connection with the limits of wave functions for quantum harmonic oscillators with high energies.
