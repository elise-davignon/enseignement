Gathered here are student evaluation reports compiled from surveys answered by students after each semester. These often include student comments regarding my teaching. I always make a point of being open to feedback, and honest with myself, and I have taken onboard much of the students' remarks through the years.

selected excerpts

 Here are a few selected excerpts of students' comments. The full reports are available below.

from MAT2717 - Stochastic processes - fall 2022

“In the beginning, I was scared of taking this class with Élise, because I had heard that her exams were difficult. Now, I’m so happy to have taken it. Élise was a small (kinda big) ray of sunshine all throughout the semester. She knew how to make us laugh, stimulate us, and make stochastic processes interesting, even at 8:30 am. I thought I was going to hate this class, but it is the complete opposite now! Élise is a wonderful person, and it is obvious that she is made to teach, which is not so common. She has a lot of love to give. Thanks for everything Élise :) !”

“Homework assignments are interesting, because they allow us to see practical applications for the material.”

“It’s Élise. Excellent teacher and we know it. My comments will probably be similar to what I have already said in other evaluations (maybe with small differences). She is passionate about her subject, and ultra-competent. She is also always ready and willing to answer questions, she is chatty and friendly. It is also cool that she takes the time to write detailed course notes. The notes are very good.”

“Élise is one of the best teachers I have had in mathematics in university.”

“The teacher brings a refreshing approach to the material and knows how to make it interesting through her homework assignments, examples and explanations. In general, I really like taking her classes.”

from MAT1720 - Introduction to probability - winter 2022

“I also wanted to tell you that, online, you were the best class I have taken; I could have taken the class entirely online and I feel like I would have gotten the same quality of teaching!!”

“Mrs. Davignon perfectly masters the subject, and the available course notes are very complete.”

“The teacher’s attitude and teaching style are very motivating. She listens to students and answers questions without judgment. This course and the teacher’s methods were a big hit for me.”

from MAT1978 - Probability and statistics for CS students - winter 2021

“The course is well tailored to the audience: computer science students. We get a good grasp of the material without getting into too much details with all the mathematical technicalities. I also liked the fact that there were lots of examples done in class because it helps a lot to understand the material in my opinion. The rhythm was perfect to assimilate the subject matter.”

“Prof. Davignon taught the class with great brio! She is able to vulgarize the subject and to make it accessible for everyone. She is extremely patient and attentive to the needs of her students. I have had a lot of fun in her class. She has a great sense of humour and makes little jokes from time to time, which makes class very enjoyable. She doesn’t hesitate to show her own weaknesses, too. It is an exceptional quality in a teacher. Seeing also the human side of the teacher, it helps to feel closer to her and create a better connection with students. I highly recommend Prof. Davignon! The university needs to make more space for young professionals like her. The quality of teaching would rise significantly, in my opinion. In two years studying at UdeM, I have never had a course with a teacher so competent, and who explains things so well! Frankly exceptional! [...] Excellent performance! I hope Prof. Davignon will be given the necessary space to help the UdeM community move forward. She should be coaching other (older) professors to bring their courses up to snuff, like hers! For her final grade, I give an A++ haha. I hope I get her in other courses!”

“Élise is a talented teacher. She clearly loves to teach. Her class is fun!”
